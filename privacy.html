Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Proofwise is built so that your habits stay yours. This page explains, in plain words, what the app stores, what leaves your phone and why.

Who is responsible

Proofwise is made by Cruz de Groot, the Netherlands. Under EU privacy law (GDPR) that makes me the “controller” for the little personal data described below. You can reach me at proofwise.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you put into Proofwise is stored only on your phone, in the app’s own database: your tasks and amounts, what you ticked off and when, your daily check-ins (how your day and energy were, what helped or got in the way, and any notes), counters, plans, your weekly focus and your settings. The home-screen and lock-screen widgets read the same data on the same phone.

This data is not sent to me or to anyone else. There is no account, no server and no cloud copy made by Proofwise. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, that backup can include Proofwise’s data, as with any app; that backup is handled by Apple under your own Apple settings.

What leaves your phone: subscriptions

Proofwise is a paid subscription with a free trial. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, email address or payment details.

To check whether your subscription or trial is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID created by the app, which is not linked to your name or Apple ID;
  • your purchase and subscription history for Proofwise (which plan, trial, renewals, cancellations), from Apple;
  • basic technical information needed to process that: app version, iOS version, device model, country and IP address.

This is needed to give you the subscription you bought (GDPR article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract). RevenueCat does not receive any of your habit or check-in data. RevenueCat processes data in the USA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

What Proofwise does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking tools.
  • No analytics services that follow you across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • Your check-in answers are never used to sell anything to you.

Notifications

Reminders, including the reminder two days before a free trial ends, are scheduled on your phone itself. They don’t go through a server. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Your rights and choices

  • See and take your data: Settings → Your data → export a full backup or a spreadsheet.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Your data → Delete all data, or delete the app.
  • Subscription data at RevenueCat: email me and I’ll have it deleted. You can also ask for access, correction or a copy.
  • Complain: you can contact the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in your own country.

Children

Proofwise is not directed at children under 13. The app doesn’t ask for anyone’s age, name or email, and the habit data stays on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Important changes are also mentioned in the app’s release notes.
