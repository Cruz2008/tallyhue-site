Subscription

How do I cancel?

In Tallyhue: Settings → Subscription → Manage or cancel. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tallyhue. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

I already paid, but the app asks me to subscribe

Tap “Restore purchases” on the subscribe screen, or Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tallyhue, and choose the other plan.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy made by Tallyhue.

How do I keep a copy?

Settings → Your data → export a full backup (one file) or a spreadsheet.

New phone?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one; Tallyhue’s data comes along with it.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data. This can’t be undone.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications for Tallyhue are on: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tallyhue. Also check Focus modes. Reminders are only sent on days you have something left to do.

The widget looks out of date

Open Tallyhue once; the widgets update right away. iOS decides how often widgets refresh on their own, so a short delay is normal.

How do I add a widget?

Press and hold an empty spot on the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → Tallyhue. For the lock screen: press and hold the lock screen → Customize.
